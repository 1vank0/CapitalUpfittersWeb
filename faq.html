How long does a commercial vehicle wrap last?

With proper care, a professionally installed commercial vehicle wrap using 3M or Avery Dennison materials lasts 5–7 years. We back every installation with a 1-year workmanship warranty covering peeling, bubbling, and color fading.

Related service

Will a vehicle wrap damage my fleet's paint?

No. A professionally installed wrap actually protects your vehicle's original paint from UV rays, minor abrasions, and environmental exposure. When removed by a professional, the original paint is preserved underneath.

What materials do you use?

What's the difference between nerf bars and running boards?

Nerf bars are round or oval steel/aluminum tubes that run along the rocker panel. Running boards are flat, wider platforms that provide a larger step surface. Both serve the same purpose — easier entry and exit — but running boards give more standing room, while nerf bars have a more rugged appearance and offer some rocker panel protection.

Related service

Do you carry PowerStep automatic running boards?

Yes. We carry and install AMP Research PowerStep, Bestop PowerBoard, and other electric/automatic running board brands. Power steps deploy when you open the door and retract when closed, giving you a completely clean look when driving. Installed pricing is quoted per vehicle once we confirm fitment.

Do I need a relay for an LED light bar?

Yes — for any high-draw LED light bar (over 50W), a proper relay and fuse setup is required. This protects your factory wiring and ensures the light gets full voltage. We include proper relay wiring on all our light bar installs.

Are LED light bars legal to use on public roads in Maryland?

Most LED light bars are DOT-legal for off-road use only when mounted on roof or bumper. Some SAE-compliant driving/fog lights are street-legal. We'll advise on compliance during the consultation and mark non-street-legal lights appropriately.

What is a Stealth Hitch?

A Stealth Hitch is a specialized receiver hitch designed to remain completely hidden when not in use — no visible receiver tube hanging down. They're custom-engineered per vehicle and are popular for luxury SUVs and vehicles where aesthetics matter. Parts and labor are quoted per vehicle after a fitment review.

Related service

Will a hitch installation void my warranty?

In most cases, no. The Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act protects consumers from dealers voiding warranties simply because an aftermarket part was installed. We use OEM-style installation methods that do not compromise your vehicle's frame or electrical system.

Will a tonneau cover fit my truck?

Tonneau covers are available for virtually all popular truck models — F-150, Silverado, Ram, Tundra, Tacoma, and more. We carry vehicle-specific covers that use the existing stake hole mounting or clamp-on systems. No drilling required. Contact us with your year, make, model, and cab/bed configuration and we'll confirm availability.

Do tonneau covers improve gas mileage?

Yes, modestly. An open truck bed creates aerodynamic drag that a tonneau cover eliminates. Independent testing has shown improvements of 2–5% in fuel economy at highway speeds, depending on driving conditions and cover type. Hard covers generally outperform soft covers for aerodynamics.

Related service

Can I still use the full bed with a tonneau cover?

It depends on the cover type. Folding covers can be pushed fully forward for full bed access. Roll-up covers also allow full-bed use when rolled up. Retractable covers lock in any position. One-piece hard covers require removal for full access. We'll help you choose the right type for how you use your truck.

Related service

Will fender flares affect my truck's aerodynamics?

OE-style fender flares have minimal aerodynamic impact since they follow the factory body contour. Cut-out/wide-body flares have a slightly larger profile but the difference in fuel economy is negligible for most drivers.

Quality grille guards from reputable brands (Go Rhino, Ranch Hand, Westin) are engineered to not interfere with factory crash sensors or airbag deployment. We only install brands that meet or exceed these standards.

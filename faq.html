What's the difference between nerf bars and running boards?

Nerf bars are round or oval steel/aluminum tubes that run along the rocker panel. Running boards are flat, wider platforms that provide a larger step surface. Both serve the same purpose — easier entry and exit — but running boards give more standing room, while nerf bars have a more rugged appearance and offer some rocker panel protection.

What is a Stealth Hitch?

A Stealth Hitch is a specialized receiver hitch designed to remain completely hidden when not in use — no visible receiver tube hanging down. Will a hitch installation void my warranty?

In most cases, no. The Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act protects consumers from dealers voiding warranties simply because an aftermarket part was installed. We use OEM-style installation methods that do not compromise your vehicle's frame or electrical system.

Do tonneau covers improve gas mileage?

Yes, modestly. An open truck bed creates aerodynamic drag that a tonneau cover eliminates. Independent testing has shown improvements of 2–5% in fuel economy at highway speeds, depending on driving conditions and cover type. Hard covers generally outperform soft covers for aerodynamics.

One-piece hard covers require removal for full access. We'll help you choose the right type for how you use your truck.

Purpose-Built Engineering

The Only Hitch Built
For Your Car.

Not every hitch belongs on every vehicle. Stealth Hitch is engineered from the ground up for luxury, electric, and performance vehicles.

Completely Hidden

The receiver tube detaches and stores away. Your vehicle looks completely stock when not towing. No visible receiver stub — factory-perfect from every angle.

Zero Permanent Modification

No cutting, no welding, no drilling into your bumper. Fully reversible factory-bolt installation. Your warranty stays intact and resale value is protected.

Lifetime Warranty

Every Stealth Hitch comes with a lifetime warranty. Engineered to meet or exceed OEM towing specs for each specific vehicle platform.

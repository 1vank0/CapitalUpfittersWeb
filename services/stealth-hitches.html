The Short Answer

What Is a Stealth Hitch?

A Stealth Hitch is a vehicle-specific hidden hitch system. Its stainless vertical latch is installed behind the bumper; the rack receiver or towing ball mount connects from below without tools and removes when you are finished. With the component removed, there is no receiver tube hanging below the bumper.

  • Factory-clean appearanceThe detachable component comes off while the structural hitch remains concealed behind the bumper.
  • Vehicle-specific engineeringCrossbeam, latch, receiver, tow components and wiring are selected for the exact supported application.
  • Premium materialsStealth describes a fully welded, powder-coated structure with a stainless latch block and integrated lock.

Designed Around the Vehicle

Why Premium-Vehicle Owners Choose a Hidden Hitch

The goal is useful carrying or towing capability without leaving a permanent-looking receiver below a carefully designed rear bumper.

01

02

03

Designed to Preserve Clearance

The system is engineered around the vehicle’s intended ground clearance, bumper structure and supported factory features.

Choose by Intended Use

Rack Package vs. Tow Package

Both packages use the vehicle-specific hidden latch structure. The detachable component—and what it is allowed to do—is different.

Rack Package
For approved bike racks and cargo carriers
  • Hidden crossbeam and locking vertical latch
  • Detachable rack receiver sized for the application
  • No trailer wiring required for rack-only use
  • Can be eligible for a later vehicle-specific tow conversion
Accessories only: the rack receiver must never be used for trailer towing.
Quote My Rack Package
Rack + Tow Package
For trailer towing plus rack and cargo use
  • Rack-package hardware for approved accessories
  • Separate detachable tow ball mount
  • Safety-chain anchors and vehicle-specific trailer wiring
  • Programming or active harness included when the fitment requires it
Towing uses the dedicated ball mount—not the square rack receiver—and is limited by the lowest-rated vehicle or towing component.

Stealth Hitch Installation FAQ

Supported Stealth Hitch applications are designed without a visible bumper cutout. Temporary fascia removal is common, and some vehicles require a concealed opening or trim to a lower panel or gravel guard. We confirm the procedure before installation.

A rack-only installation normally does not need trailer wiring. Tow packages use vehicle-specific wiring, and some BMW, Audi, Porsche and Volvo applications can require active harnesses or programming. We verify requirements by year, model, trim and VIN.

Capacity is vehicle- and application-specific. The safe limit is the lowest rating among the vehicle, hitch, tow ball or mount, trailer and other components. A hitch cannot increase the vehicle manufacturer’s tow rating.

Stealth systems are designed to preserve factory appearance, ground clearance and supported vehicle features. Sensor behavior and foot-swipe placement can vary while a detachable component is installed, so we check applicable functions during final quality control.

Questions about your particular vehicle?
Talk to our team: (301) 304-1419

Will installing a Stealth Hitch void my warranty?

Vehicle warranty coverage depends on the applicable terms and the cause of any issue. We cannot promise that every claim is covered. Some installations require unseen underbody trimming; review your vehicle and hitch warranty terms.

Can I tow with a Stealth Hitch?

Only with the correct towing configuration and within the lowest applicable vehicle, hitch and accessory rating. Never tow with the square rack receiver. Stealth Hitches does not support weight-distribution or anti-sway systems. We confirm the part, ball mount and wiring for your vehicle.

Do I need to remove the bumper?

Bumper fascia removal and trim work depend on the vehicle and hitch. We review the installation instructions for your exact part and explain the work in your quote.

How long does installation take?

Installation time depends on vehicle access, trim work, wiring and the selected kit. We confirm the estimated time and appointment availability with your quote.

Is the receiver visible when not in use?

No. The receiver tube detaches and stores in your cargo area. When removed, the hitch beam sits fully behind the bumper — completely invisible. Your vehicle looks factory stock.

Does Tesla Model Y need bumper removal?

Requirements vary by model year and hitch kit. Send your Model Y year and trim so we can confirm the correct installation procedure and labor.

What's the difference between the Rack Only and Combo Package?

Rack Only is for compatible accessories, never trailer towing. A towing configuration uses the appropriate towing kit, ball mount and wiring. Tell us what you plan to carry or tow so we can verify the configuration instead of guessing.

Do you have my vehicle's hitch in stock?

Availability varies by part number. Call us at (301) 304-1419 to check availability for your specific year and model.

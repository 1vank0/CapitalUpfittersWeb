Explore manufacturer guidance

Hidden, Stealth or Invisi?

Hidden conceals the crosstube while leaving the receiver visible. The EcoHitch Stealth design uses a removable receiver. Invisi conceals the receiver behind a license plate or factory panel when not in use. These features depend on the specific part.

EcoHitch’s Stealth design is a Torklift product style, distinct from the Stealth Hitches brand.

Choose the use. Verify the limits.

Receiver size, accessory clearance, tongue weight and towing limits all matter. Towing also requires the appropriate ball mount and wiring. Stay within the lowest applicable vehicle, hitch and accessory rating.

Front-mounted receivers need their own fitment and use review. A rear towing rating is not a front-receiver rating.

What is the difference between Hidden, Stealth and Invisi?

Hidden conceals the crosstube while leaving the receiver visible. The EcoHitch Stealth style has a removable receiver. Invisi conceals the receiver behind a license plate or factory panel when not in use. Available designs depend on the vehicle-specific part. EcoHitch Stealth is distinct from the Stealth Hitches brand.

Does an EcoHitch need drilling?

Installation requirements depend on your vehicle and the selected part. We review the manufacturer instructions for drilling, trimming, fascia removal and wiring before confirming the work.

How much can an EcoHitch tow?

Ratings depend on the vehicle and exact hitch part. We review the vehicle limits, hitch rating, ball mount, wiring and planned load. Never exceed the lowest applicable rating; a hitch does not increase your vehicle’s towing capacity.

Can I use a bike rack or cargo carrier on it?

We check the receiver size, accessory fit, clearance and combined accessory-and-cargo weight against the applicable limits. Share your rack or carrier details so we can confirm compatibility.

Which vehicles does EcoHitch fit?

Fitment is vehicle- and part-specific. Send your year, make, model, trim and intended use so we can check available front or rear options. Entering your vehicle here does not confirm compatibility.

What warranty does EcoHitch have?

Warranty coverage and conditions depend on the product and applicable manufacturer terms. We can help identify the terms for the quoted part; do not assume that all parts or installation work share the same coverage.

Can I request installation if I already own the hitch?

Yes. Choose “I already have the part” and include the part number if available. The shop will review compatibility, completeness and installation requirements before quoting labor.

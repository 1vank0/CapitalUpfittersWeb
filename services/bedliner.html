Why Patriot Liner

Tough protection.
A premium finish.

Not all spray liners are the same. The chemistry, surface preparation and application method make a difference. Patriot Liner combines polyurea protection with the clean, uniform finish your truck deserves.

01

Preparation that matters.

The bed is cleaned, sanded and masked. Proper preparation supports adhesion and a clean edge line, with rust, damage and old coatings addressed before spraying.

02

Sprayed to fit your bed.

A spray-applied coating follows the contours of the bed rather than sitting inside it as a loose insert. We plan around tie-downs, rails and other accessories for a consistent finish.

03

Built for work. Finished with care.

From daily hauling to weekend projects, your liner should look as good as it works. We inspect the finish and explain loading, care and applicable warranty terms at pickup.

Before you book

Common questions.

Get to know the product, the preparation and what happens when you bring your truck to us.

How much does a spray-on bedliner cost?

Patriot Liner starts at $595 plus tax for compact and standard beds up to 6.5 feet. Long-bed pricing starts at $795, with tax confirmed in your written quote. Prices assume a clean bed with no accessories. Final pricing may differ based on bed condition, existing coatings and preparation required. Premium UV+ is an optional $200 upgrade.

How long does installation take?

The appointment includes inspection, preparation, masking, spraying and a final check. We confirm your drop-off and pickup timing when booking, based on bed condition and the work selected. Ask about cure and loading instructions before using the bed.

What sets Patriot Liner apart?

Patriot Liner combines polyurea protection with a clean, uniform finish. Its spray-applied coating follows the shape of the prepared bed rather than fitting as a separate drop-in shell. Careful surface preparation and application are essential. For stronger protection against harsh chemicals and UV rays, ask about the Premium UV+ upgrade with its pure-black finish.

Can I add a bed cover at the same time?

Yes. Tell us which cover you want and we can quote the liner and cover together, checking the mounting rails, bed dimensions and any existing accessories. Compare Westin, BAKFlip, TruXedo and Retrax on our bed cover page.

What warranty applies?

Ask us for the current Patriot Liner manufacturer warranty terms for your application and the shop labor terms before authorizing the work. Coverage, exclusions and care requirements should be confirmed in writing; not every surface or use has the same coverage.

Do you offer custom colors or coatings beyond truck beds?

Black is the standard bedliner color. Ask about custom colors, product suitability and lead time. We also review trailer floors, cargo vans, steps, marine surfaces and industrial equipment. Surface condition and intended use determine which preparation and coating are appropriate.

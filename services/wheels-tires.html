The details that matter

A size is not
a fitment check.

We review the complete combination before recommending parts. Your vehicle manufacturer’s requirements are the starting point.

Wheels: more than diameter

Bolt pattern, hub fit, width, offset, load rating and brake clearance all need review. A matching bolt pattern alone does not establish compatibility.

Tires: size and ratings together

The tire must suit the wheel and meet vehicle requirements for load and speed ratings. Clearance must be checked through steering and suspension travel, not just while parked.

Changes affect the whole vehicle

Share any lift, leveling kit, brake upgrade or existing modification. Ask about TPMS compatibility, alignment needs and the effects of a size change before ordering.

Before you choose.

Questions about an existing setup?
Talk to our team.

Will 35-inch tires fit my truck?

Tire diameter alone cannot confirm fit. Vehicle trim, wheel width and offset, suspension, brake clearance and body clearance all matter. Share the exact tire and wheel specifications before ordering; a lift height is not a fitment guarantee.

Can I keep my current wheels?

Possibly. The tire must be approved for the wheel width and diameter, meet the vehicle requirements and clear the vehicle throughout its operating range. We review the combination before quoting.

What if I already own wheels or tires?

Tell us what you have and include the part numbers and sizes in your request. We will review compatibility, condition and the work needed before confirming whether we can install them.

Does this request reserve parts or an appointment?

No. The shop first reviews your vehicle and plans, then confirms parts, labor, availability and scheduling. No payment is collected on this page.

Manual Running Boards & Steps
Tube Steps & Nerf Bars

Round or oval steel/aluminum tube steps in gloss black, chrome, or textured finishes. The most popular and most affordable option for trucks and SUVs.

Power Running Boards

Automatic retractable steps (AMP Research PowerStep, Bestop PowerBoard) that deploy when the door opens and retract while driving. Premium look, maximum convenience.

Flat Running Boards

Wide, flat platform boards in aluminum or composite. Larger step surface than nerf bars — ideal for families, taller vehicles, or anyone who wants a more substantial step.

Rock Sliders

Heavy-duty steel sliders that protect your rocker panels from trail damage while providing a step. Popular for lifted trucks and off-road builds.

Powered / Electric Running Boards

One of the best-selling electric running boards in North America. Deploys automatically when your door opens — retracts flush with the body when you close it. No step visible when driving. Factory-integrated look, Plug-N-Play install on most trucks.

Our Process

Bolted on clean.

1
Vehicle Lookup

We confirm your exact year, make, model, and cab configuration to select the right bracket hardware and step product.

2
Fitment Check

We verify the rocker mounting points and check for any accessories (lift kits, flares) that may require specific hardware or offsets.

3
Professional Install

Torqued to spec using factory-compatible hardware. Wiring harness connected for power steps. Vehicle-specific mounting requirements confirmed before work begins.

4
Level & Pickup

Final alignment check, test of operation (power steps), and delivery. Shop time is confirmed with your installation quote.

Nerf bars are round or oval tubes that run along the rocker panel — they look rugged and provide a small step. Running boards are flat, wider platforms that give significantly more standing surface. Both improve entry and exit from lifted or tall vehicles. Running boards are better for daily use; nerf bars offer a sportier look. We'll help you choose based on how you use the truck.

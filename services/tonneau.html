Will a tonneau cover fit my truck?

Fitment depends on your truck’s year, make, model, bed length and existing accessories. We check the exact cover application and installation requirements before confirming your quote. A product selection is not a compatibility guarantee.

Are tonneau covers waterproof?

A properly installed cover helps manage rain and protect cargo, but no cover should be treated as a completely waterproof enclosure. Seals, drainage, bed shape and driving conditions all matter. Keep water-sensitive valuables in suitable containers.

Can I still use the full bed with a tonneau cover?

Access depends on the design. Soft covers roll toward the cab. Hard-folding covers stack or fold upright, depending on the model. Retractable covers store in a canister at the front of the bed. We can help compare the space tradeoffs for what you carry.

Do tonneau covers improve gas mileage?

Do not choose a cover based on a guaranteed fuel-economy improvement. Results depend on your truck, cover and driving conditions. Choose the access, appearance and cargo protection that suit your use.

How long does installation take?

Timing depends on the cover, your truck and any existing accessories or bedliner. We confirm parts availability, installation requirements and an estimated appointment duration with your quote.

Can I combine a bed cover with a spray-on bedliner?

Yes, you can request both services. We review cover fitment, rail clearance and the installation sequence together. Add Spray-On Bedliner in the quote form if you want a combined estimate.

Am I buying the cover when I submit?

No. This is a request for a written installed-price quote, not checkout or a reserved appointment. Our team confirms fitment, parts, labor and availability before you decide how to proceed.

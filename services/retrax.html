Our process

Clean fit, no damage.

1
Confirm the fit

We check your year, make, model, cab configuration and bed length, plus any toolbox or rails that affect the cover.

2
Choose your line

ONE or PRO, MX or XR: we match the cover to how you use your bed.

3
Install and adjust

Mounted with clamps or stake-hole hardware, no drilling on most trucks. We check the rails and canister for a tight, even fit.

4
Demo and pickup

We walk you through opening, closing and locking it. Most tonneau installs take 1–2 hours.

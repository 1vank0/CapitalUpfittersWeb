Upfit Done Right

Why upfit your work van?

A planned cargo layout pays for itself in time saved at every stop, tools protected in transit, and a safer cab. Here's what a real upfit changes.

Stay Organized

Stop climbing over cargo. Shelving, drawers and modules put every tool within reach of the side door — saving minutes on every job.

Theft Protection

Lockable cabinets, drawers and rear-door security keep your equipment where you left it. Partitions also protect the cab from shifting loads.

Protect Your Tools

Vibration and road shock are what actually kill tools in a van. Lined shelves, drawer systems and tie-downs keep equipment from riding loose.

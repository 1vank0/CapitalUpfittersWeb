FAQ

Common questions.

Step bars are tube-style bars under the doors. Running boards are wider boards with a flat step surface. Drop steps sit lower than the rocker panel for easier entry. We help you choose based on how you use your vehicle.

E-Series step bars are an entry-level 3-inch option. Pro Traxx step bars come in 4, 5 and 6-inch oval profiles. HDX drop steps are set lower for easier entry, and Sure-Grip boards add a slip-resistant rubber step area.

Westin step products come in black powder coat, chrome, textured black and stainless steel, depending on the line. We confirm the finishes available for your vehicle when we quote.

Westin mounting kits use vehicle-specific brackets. We confirm your year, make, model and cab configuration before we order.

Westin makes steps for vans, including the Mercedes Sprinter and Ford Transit. Ask us about your van when you request pricing.

Warranty terms depend on the product and finish. Westin lists chrome and polished products with a 5-year limited warranty, and black powder-coat products with 12 months. We confirm the exact coverage for your steps when we quote.

Coating Applications

PROTECTION FOR EVERY SURFACE.

From a single cargo van floor to a full municipal fleet — we apply the right coating system for your surface, environment, and performance requirement.

Blast Mitigation

Spray-applied high-elongation polyurea coatings that absorb and dissipate blast energy across vehicle panels, facility walls, and structural surfaces. Used by military, law enforcement, and government facilities requiring enhanced structural resilience.

Corrosion & Rust Protection

Seamless, pinhole-free barrier coatings that seal metal surfaces against moisture, salt, and chemical exposure. Ideal for commercial vehicle undercarriages, cargo trailers, utility equipment, and industrial steel structures in the DMV's harsh seasonal environment.

Anti-Skid & Non-Slip Surfaces

OSHA and ADA-compliant textured coatings for vehicle cargo floors, dock ramps, industrial walkways, stairs, and platforms. Maintains grip in wet, oily, or high-traffic conditions without sacrificing durability.

Structural Protection

High-build protective coating systems for structural steel, concrete, and facility surfaces requiring long-term protection from impact, abrasion, chemical exposure, and environmental degradation. Suitable for bridges, loading docks, containment areas, and municipal infrastructure.

Vehicle & Fleet Coating

Full vehicle and fleet coating programs for commercial operators and government agencies. Cargo van floors, truck beds, trailer interiors, heavy equipment surfaces — standardized application across your entire fleet for consistent protection and appearance.

Coating Chemistries

THE RIGHT CHEMISTRY FOR THE JOB.

Not every surface needs the same coating. We select the correct chemistry based on your surface type, environment, load requirements, and performance specifications.

Polyurea
Fastest Cure · Highest Flexibility

Pure spray-applied polyurea delivers 100% solids, zero VOC, and cure times measured in seconds. Exceptional tensile strength and elongation — ideal for surfaces requiring impact resistance, waterproofing, and chemical resistance in demanding environments.

Best for
Vehicle floors, trailer beds, blast-rated surfaces, containment liners
Aliphatic Topcoats
UV-Stable · Color-Retaining

Aliphatic formulations are engineered for exterior surfaces where UV exposure would degrade standard coatings. Color-stable and gloss-retaining long after aromatic systems would fade — the correct choice for any surface exposed to direct sunlight.

Best for
Exterior vehicle panels, marine surfaces, roofing, color-critical applications
Aromatic Base Systems
High Performance · Cost-Effective

Aromatic polyurea provides the highest mechanical performance — tensile strength, elongation, and abrasion resistance — at the most cost-effective price point. Typically used as the primary protective layer under an aliphatic topcoat on exterior applications.

Best for
Interior floors, containment, blast mitigation, base coats under aliphatic systems
Epoxy Systems
Chemical Resistant · Rigid Bond

Two-component epoxy coatings provide an extremely rigid, chemically resistant bond to concrete, steel, and industrial substrates. Excellent adhesion primer and base for polyurea topcoats on concrete floors and industrial surfaces.

Best for
Concrete floors, chemical containment, industrial facilities, primer/base applications

Our Process

HOW IT WORKS.

A systematic approach from first contact to final coat. Every job documented and done to spec.

1
Site Assessment

We evaluate your surfaces, environment, load requirements, and performance specs. In-shop or on-site assessment depending on scale.

2
System Selection

We specify the correct chemistry (polyurea, aliphatic, aromatic, epoxy) and application method for your surface and conditions.

3
Surface Preparation

Proper prep is everything. We clean, abrade, and prime surfaces to ensure maximum adhesion and coating longevity.

4
Application & Inspection

Coating applied to spec. Thickness gauged. Quality check before handoff. Documentation provided on request.

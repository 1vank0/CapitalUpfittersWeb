02 / Set the right expectations

Care for the finish.
Know the limits.

01

Start with the surface.

Coating does not remove existing swirls or scratches. Paint assessment comes first, with correction quoted separately when needed.

02

Make maintenance easier.

A hydrophobic coating encourages water to bead and can make routine cleaning easier. Regular washing and prompt removal of contaminants still matter.

03

Match protection to the job.

Ceramic coating is not a substitute for impact protection. For road-debris concerns, ask about PPF coverage. Product life and any warranty depend on the selected system and its terms.

03 / From assessment to aftercare

The finish starts before the coating.

    Confirm pickup timing and receive washing and maintenance guidance for your chosen coating.

FAQ

Common questions.

Longevity depends on the selected coating, preparation, exposure and maintenance. We’ll confirm the specific product, care requirements and any applicable warranty terms with your quote. No single lifespan applies to every coating.

Surface decontamination is included. Paint correction to address swirls, scratches or oxidation is assessed and quoted separately based on the paint’s condition.

Ceramic coatings bond to a prepared surface and provide hydrophobic protection. Wax is a different surface treatment with different maintenance needs. Neither replaces regular washing, and ceramic coating is not paint protection film.

Follow the cure and washing instructions supplied for your chosen product. Timing varies by coating and conditions. We’ll provide aftercare guidance at pickup so you know when and how to wash your vehicle.

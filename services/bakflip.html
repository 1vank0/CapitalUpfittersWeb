Our process

Clean fit, no damage.

1
Confirm the fit

We check your bed size, cab configuration and any toolbox or rails that affect the cover.

2
Choose your model

MX4, EZ-Lift or TS: we match the model to how you use your truck.

Install and seal

Mounted with clamps or stake-hole hardware, no drilling on most trucks. We adjust the seals for a tight, even fit.

Demo and pickup

We walk you through opening, latching and adjusting it. Most installs take 1–2 hours.

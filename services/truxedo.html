Our process

No drilling. Good fit.

1
Confirm the fit

We check your year, make, model, cab configuration and bed length, plus any toolbox or rails that affect the cover.

2
Choose your line

Soft or hard, Pro X15 or Deuce: we match the cover to how you use your bed.

3
Install and adjust

Mounted with clamps, with no drilling. We adjust the tension and seals for a tight, even fit.

4
Demo and pickup

We walk you through opening, closing and locking it. Most tonneau installs take 1–2 hours.

FAQ

Common questions.

Soft roll-ups like the Pro X15 and Lo Pro use fabric over a frame. Hard roll-ups like the Sentry use aluminum slats under the surface. We match the cover to what you carry and how you use your truck.

The Pro X15 is a soft roll-up with 15-degree rails for a low-profile look. The Lo Pro is a soft roll-up with a trigger latch. The Deuce adds a hinged front section, and the Sentry is a hard roll-up with a center security latch.

The Deuce has a front section that folds up for access to a toolbox or front cargo while the rear stays covered. It also rolls up fully for large loads.

BAKFlip vs. Retrax for Maryland winters.

2026-10-31

Capital Upfitters Team · October 2026 6 min read

Winter decides more about a bed cover than most buyers expect. Freezing rain, salt brine on every road and a few weeks below 20°F put a cover through things a dry July never does. The two covers we get asked about most are the BAKFlip MX4, a hard folding cover, and Retrax retractables. Here is an honest comparison of how each handles a Maryland winter, and where each falls short.

The short answer

Neither cover wins everywhere. Choose the BAKFlip MX4 if you want rigid panels, an auto-latch on every panel and the ability to fold the cover forward for tall loads. Choose a Retrax if you want to stop the cover partway open for partial loads, or you want options like T-slot rails (XR) or an electric version. In winter, the real difference is how each one reacts to ice and salt.

Not sure which fits? Tell us your truck and what you haul. We confirm fitment before we order, and we will tell you if a different cover suits you better.

How each cover works

The BAKFlip MX4 is a hard folding cover. Its polymer-reinforced aluminum panels fold toward the cab, a patented auto-latch locks every panel when closed, and it is rated for 400 lbs of evenly distributed load. BAKFlip tests it from −40°F to 180°F and backs it with a 5-year warranty.

Retrax covers are retractable: the slats retract at the cab and the cover locks in any open position. The RetraxONE line uses polycarbonate and the RetraxPRO line uses double-wall aluminum slats. MX versions sit flush with low-profile rails, and XR versions add T-slot rails for crossbars and racks. We list the full lineup on our Retrax page.

What a Maryland winter does to a bed cover

Freezing rain and ice

Freezing rain can glaze the seams, seals and rails of any cover. Do not force a cover that is frozen shut. Let it thaw or clear the ice first, because forcing it is how seals tear and latches break.

The designs fail differently. A retractable has rollers, slats and a canister, so ice in the rails or at the canister can bind the cover. The sealed rollers on the RetraxPRO MX are designed to resist freezing and binding. A folding cover has fewer sliding parts, but ice across the panel seams can stop the panels from folding until it melts.

Freeze and thaw cycles

DMV winters often swing above and below freezing within a day. The usual winter problem is meltwater that seeps into seams and rails, then refreezes overnight. Wipe the seals dry, cycle the cover once during a thaw, and keep the rails and any drain paths clear of leaves and grit. Both covers handle water at the rails, so debris there causes trouble on either one.

Road salt and brine

Salt brine settles into hinges, rails, fasteners and seals on any cover. Aluminum panels and slats do not rust, but the hardware around them can corrode. Rinse the cover and the bed rails after salted roads and dry the moving parts when you can. The same salt is working on the rest of your truck, which is why we also cover when to undercoat your truck in Maryland.

Cold and snow

Cold stiffens seals on every cover. BAKFlip publishes a −40°F rating for the MX4. For a Retrax, we confirm the specs for your model when we quote. Brush snow off before you open any cover, and do not let a heavy snow load sit on it. Load ratings are for evenly distributed cargo, not a substitute for sweeping the cover.

Access, cargo and security

With the BAKFlip you can fold the panels forward to use the bed, and flip them up for tall loads. With a Retrax you open only as much of the bed as you need and lock the cover where it stops. That helps with partial loads, though the canister takes some bed length at the cab. Ask us what that means for your bed.

On security, the BAKFlip auto-latches every panel. Retrax covers latch or key-lock along the rail. No tonneau cover makes a bed theft-proof, so do not leave valuables in sight or in the bed.

Models within each brand

The BAKFlip MX4 also comes in EZ-Lift and TS versions, which we compare on the BAKFlip page. Retrax comes in ONE and PRO, MX and XR, plus electric Powertrax models that open with a key fob. A key fob is nice on a cold driveway, but ice still matters, so the same winter habits apply. You can see every option side by side on our tonneau covers page.

The BAKFlip MX4 carries a 5-year warranty. Retrax warranty terms differ by line, so we confirm the coverage for the model you choose when we quote. Most tonneau installs take 1–2 hours at our Rockville shop, with no drilling on most trucks.

So which one should you choose?

  • Choose the BAKFlip MX4 if you want rigid, auto-latching panels and you often haul tall loads.
  • Choose a Retrax if you carry partial loads often, or you want T-slot rails or an electric cover.
  • Ask us first if you have a toolbox, rails or a short bed. Those details can decide it.

Common questions

Which is better for a Maryland winter, BAKFlip or Retrax?

Neither wins outright. BAKFlip MX4 is a rigid hard folding cover tested from -40°F to 180°F, with fewer sliding parts. A Retrax retractable lets you stop the cover partway open and, on PRO models, uses double-wall aluminum slats. We match the cover to what you haul and how you park.

Can ice freeze a bed cover shut?

Yes. Freezing rain can glaze the seams, seals and rails of any cover. Do not force a frozen cover. Let it thaw or clear the ice first, because forcing it is how seals tear and latches break.

Does road salt damage a tonneau cover?

Salt brine can build up around hinges, rails, fasteners and seals on any cover. Rinse the cover and the bed rails after salted roads, and dry moving parts when you can.

Can I haul tall cargo with each cover?

With a BAKFlip MX4 you fold the panels forward for open-bed loads. With a Retrax you retract the cover toward the cab. Both give you more room than a closed cover, but the canister on a retractable takes some bed length, so ask us about your truck.

Will either cover fit my truck?

Both brands make vehicle-specific covers. We confirm your year, make, model, cab configuration and bed length, plus any toolbox or rails, before we order.

The process

What actually happens to your truck

  1. Surface prep & masking

    Bed, rails, and adjacent panels are cleaned, scuffed, and masked so overspray never touches paint you didn't ask us to coat.

  2. Heated polyurea application

    Patriot Liner material is applied heated to roughly 155°F at 2,000 PSI for consistent thickness and adhesion.

  3. Cure & inspection

    The coating cures before the truck leaves the bay. We walk the finish with you before pickup.

Bedliner FAQ

How long does installation take?+

Most spray-in bedliner installations take 2–3 hours from drop-off to pickup. Morning drop-off usually means same-afternoon pickup.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing starts at $575 for compact trucks (up to 5 ft bed), $595 for standard beds (up to 6.5 ft), $635 for HD trucks, and $675 for long beds (8 ft). A Premium UV+ add-on is available for +$200. All prices plus tax.

Do you offer custom bedliner colors?+

Yes. Patriot Liner is available in custom colors beyond standard black — ask about options for your specific truck.

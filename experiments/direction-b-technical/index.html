Vehicle Fitment

Does this fit my vehicle?

We don't run a full online configurator — vehicle-specific fitment on hitches, running boards, and tonneau covers gets confirmed by a person before anything is ordered. Here's what that check covers:

  • Hitch class and receiver type for your exact year/make/model
  • Frame mounting points and any existing modifications
  • Wiring harness pin-out (4-pin vs. 7-pin) for trailer lighting
  • Bed dimensions and rail type for tonneau covers or running boards

Reference

Hitch classes, at a glance

Standard SAE hitch classifications — useful context before your fitment check, not a substitute for one.

Class Receiver Typical GTW* Common use
Class I 1.25" Up to 2,000 lbs Bike racks, small utility trailers — sedans & crossovers
Class II 1.25" Up to 3,500 lbs Small trailers, cargo carriers — crossovers & minivans
Class III 2" Up to 8,000 lbs Boats, campers, mid-size trailers — trucks & SUVs
Class IV 2" Up to 10,000 lbs Heavier trailers, work equipment — full-size trucks
Class V 2" / 2.5" Up to 20,000+ lbs Commercial & heavy-duty towing — HD pickups
Stealth Hitch Concealed Vehicle-dependent Luxury SUVs & EVs where a visible receiver isn't wanted
*Gross Trailer Weight (GTW) figures are general SAE class references, not a quote for any specific vehicle. Your actual tow rating depends on the vehicle's factory towing package — confirmed during your fitment check.

Questions

Frequently asked, answered plainly.

We confirm hitch class, mounting points, and part number against your exact year, make, and model before anything is ordered — either by VIN or year/make/model. If a fitment question can't be resolved from the shop, we say so up front rather than guessing.
In most cases, no. The Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act prevents a dealer from voiding your factory warranty solely because a non-OEM part was installed, as long as that part didn't cause the failure being claimed. We install to manufacturer torque spec and document the install for exactly this reason.
Yes, for most services. Customer-supplied-parts labor is billed at a different rate than shop-supplied parts and labor, since we're not sourcing or warrantying the part itself. Ask for specifics when you request a quote.

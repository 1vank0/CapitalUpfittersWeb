Which class fits your towing need?

Standard SAE hitch classifications. Your vehicle's actual rating depends on its factory towing package — confirmed during your fitment check, not assumed from this table.

Vehicle Fitment

Does a hitch fit my vehicle?

Before we quote a firm price, we confirm the right hitch class and part number for your exact year, make, and model. This isn't a full online configurator — it's a real check, done by a person, before anything is ordered.

  • Hitch class and receiver type confirmed against your VIN or year/make/model
  • Frame mounting points inspected for existing modifications or damage
  • Wiring harness pin-out confirmed (4-pin vs. 7-pin) for trailer lighting
  • Stealth Hitch candidacy confirmed for luxury/EV applications

Warranty & Documentation

What "installed to spec" actually protects.

Magnuson-Moss Protection

Federal law prevents a dealer from voiding your factory warranty solely because a non-OEM part was installed — as long as that part didn't cause the failure being claimed.

Documented Install

Torque values and install date are recorded at the time of service — useful reference if a warranty question comes up later.

Manufacturer Part Warranty

Hitch hardware carries its own manufacturer warranty, separate from installation labor. Terms vary by brand — ask at quote time.

# Capital Upfitters — Client and Brand Brief

## Client snapshot

- Business: Capital Upfitters, a family-owned vehicle upfitting and protective-coatings shop in Rockville, Maryland.
- Core audiences: personal truck/SUV owners, commercial fleet managers, dealer and government buyers, and industrial-coatings clients.
- Primary conversion: start a quote. Secondary conversions: choose an audience path, find vehicle-compatible services, call the shop, or explore a specific service.
- Existing brand assets: Capital Upfitters wordmark, obsidian/navy base, Manrope/Inter type pairing, vehicle-fit selector, service and audience route structure.

## Positioning

Capital Upfitters should feel like the precise, accountable local specialist for premium personal builds and mission-focused commercial work. The visual system should communicate craft, fitment confidence, material quality, and operational readiness without looking like a loud aftermarket catalog.

## Visual direction

- Theme: restrained automotive editorial.
- Backgrounds: obsidian `#090b0e`, graphite `#13171c`, porcelain `#f3f1ed`.
- Accent: aged metal `#b8955b`, used sparingly for rules, arrows, and focus details.
- Typography: Manrope for display and Inter for body/UI. Sentence case for headings and cards; uppercase only for compact 11–12px utility labels.
- Imagery: coherent AI-generated campaign photography in black, graphite, and titanium with controlled workshop lighting.
- Disclosure: generated imagery is illustrative and must never be described as completed customer work.

## Guardrails

- Preserve every existing lead route, phone action, audience query parameter, and service destination.
- Do not invent reviews, certifications, client names, government relationships, or project outcomes.
- Do not use agency markings, readable plates, manufacturer logos, or third-party trademarks in generated media.
- Keep private lead-upload media separate from public homepage/gallery media.
- Store media metadata centrally; homepage markup may reference semantic media keys only.

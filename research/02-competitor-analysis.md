# Homepage Pattern Analysis

Date: 2026-08-05

## Method

This scoped redesign compares the current Capital Upfitters production homepage, the user-selected Capital Upfitters Next.js production reference, and the module grammar associated with Apple product marketing. The comparison focuses on first-viewport clarity, typography, card anatomy, image roles, section rhythm, conversion continuity, and responsive feasibility. No competitive performance or business claims are inferred from presentation alone.

## Current homepage

Strengths:

- Complete conversion surface with quote, phone, audience, service, and fitment routes.
- Familiar Manrope/Inter foundation and a compact vehicle-fit selector.
- Strong information coverage for retail, fleet, dealer/government, and industrial audiences.

Weaknesses:

- External autoplay hero video costs bandwidth/CPU and the homepage script fades important copy almost completely after inactivity.
- Audience, service, and why-us modules are gradients or placeholders instead of credible visual proof.
- Positive letter spacing and all-caps card titles make words feel cramped and produce awkward stacking.
- Repeated card grids and oversized vertical spacing make the page substantially longer than the reference.
- Bright signal-blue fields conflict with the requested restrained luxury tone.

## Next.js reference

Strengths:

- Cinematic left-copy/right-vehicle hero.
- Compact fit finder immediately after the hero.
- Image-led audience and service mosaics with varied proportions.
- Shorter page rhythm and clearer visual hierarchy.

Weaknesses for this brief:

- Saturated amber accents and hard industrial typography are more aggressive than Apple-aligned luxury.
- Square, dense modules need softer radii, tighter copy, and more consistent inner spacing.
- Media paths are still embedded in application source rather than centrally authored.

## Apple-aligned pattern translation

- Short module grammar: clear headline, one supporting sentence, one or two actions, one dominant visual.
- Tight headline tracking with generous body leading.
- Large stable media frames with minimal borders and almost no decorative shadow.
- Repeated visual language but varied section scale and image-to-text ratio.
- Color restraint: broad neutral fields and one quiet material accent rather than large saturated CTA bands.

## Limitations

- The Apple comparison is directional, not a pixel clone or endorsement.
- The user-selected reference is a related Capital Upfitters application, not an independent competitor.
- Generated media remains illustrative until replaced with approved project photography.

## Patterns of the Top 10%

1. One unmistakable first-viewport offer with a readable primary action.
2. Product/service imagery performs most of the emotional work; copy stays concise.
3. Card padding and type rhythm are tokens, not one-off adjustments.
4. Section shapes change while the grid, palette, and media treatment remain coherent.
5. Responsive assets are selected by purpose, viewport, and crop rather than downloaded at one oversized width.
6. Media metadata, disclosure, alt text, and focal points are managed in one authoritative registry.

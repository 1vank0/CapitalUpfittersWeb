---
question: What is a Stealth Hitch?
sortOrder: 19
---
A Stealth Hitch is a vehicle-specific hidden receiver hitch that tucks completely out of sight when not in use — no visible tube hanging below your bumper. They're designed for luxury SUVs and vehicles where aesthetics matter. Stealth Hitches start at $1,000 for parts and labor and are available for most popular SUV models.

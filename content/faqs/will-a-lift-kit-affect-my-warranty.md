---
question: Will a lift kit affect my warranty?
sortOrder: 43
---
Lift kits installed by certified professionals using quality parts won't void your powertrain warranty under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act. However, if a lift-related component causes a failure, that component's coverage may be affected. We use quality branded kits (ReadyLIFT, Rough Country, BDS) to minimize this risk.

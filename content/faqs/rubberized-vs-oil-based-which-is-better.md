question: Rubberized vs. oil-based — which is better?
sortOrder: 54
---
Both serve different purposes and work best together. Rubberized undercoating is ideal for flat exposed surfaces — it creates a thick, permanent rubber barrier. Oil-based (fluid film) penetrates into seams, crevices, and frame cavities that rubber can't reach. Many customers opt for both for maximum protection. We'll recommend the right approach for your vehicle.

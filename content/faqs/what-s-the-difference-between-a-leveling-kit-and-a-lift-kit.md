---
question: What's the difference between a leveling kit and a lift kit?
sortOrder: 44
---
A leveling kit only raises the front end to match the rear height — typically 1.5″–3″ of lift. A lift kit raises the entire vehicle, front and rear, for true additional ground clearance. Leveling kits are simpler, cheaper, and faster to install. Lift kits offer more capability but require more parts and labor.

question: What's the difference between nerf bars and running boards?
sortOrder: 38
---
Nerf bars are round or oval tubes that run along the rocker panel — they look rugged and provide a small step. Running boards are flat, wider platforms that give significantly more standing surface. Both improve entry and exit from lifted or tall vehicles. Running boards are better for daily use; nerf bars offer a sportier look. We'll help you choose based on how you use the truck.

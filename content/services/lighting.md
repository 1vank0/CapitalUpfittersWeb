## WHY UPGRADE TO LED LIGHTING?

Factory lighting wasn't built for job sites, off-road trails, or long rural drives after dark. Here's what a quality LED upgrade changes.

LED light bars throw 10–20× more usable light than factory fog lights. Essential for rural driving, construction sites, and off-road use after dark.

Work lights mounted on trucks, vans, and equipment let your crew work safely after dark. Required on many commercial job sites and government contracts.

Quality LED lights last 50,000+ hours with minimal power draw. No bulbs to replace. No heat damage to housings.

## LIGHTING SYSTEMS WE INSTALL.

From single off-road light bars to full fleet lighting packages — we spec, supply, mount, and wire it all.

Single-row and dual-row LED light bars for off-road use, rural driving, and brush clearing. Roof mounts, bumper mounts, and grille mounts available. RIGID Industries D-Series and E-Series are the industry standard.

Scene lights, cargo lights, and underbody work lights for commercial vans, utility trucks, and service vehicles. Popular with HVAC, electrical, and emergency service fleets across the DMV. We install and wire to factory switch panels or custom switches.

Grille-mount pod lights, bumper bar inserts, and rock lights for visibility and off-road use. A growing request from first responders and outdoor enthusiasts in the DMV region.

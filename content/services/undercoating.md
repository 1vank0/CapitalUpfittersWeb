faqs:
  - question: When's the best time to undercoat?
    answer: >-
      The best time is when the vehicle is new, before any salt exposure —
      ideally before the first winter. If the vehicle is used, undercoating is
      still effective as long as the rust hasn't progressed to structural
      damage. We do undercoating year-round — fall and spring are popular, but
      any time works.
  - question: Rubberized vs. oil-based — which is better?
    answer: >-
      Both serve different purposes and work best together. Rubberized
      undercoating is ideal for flat exposed surfaces — it creates a thick,
      permanent rubber barrier. Oil-based (fluid film) penetrates into seams,
      crevices, and frame cavities that rubber can't reach. Many customers opt
      for both for maximum protection. We'll recommend the right approach for
      your vehicle.
  - question: Can you undercoat a vehicle that already has rust?
    answer: >-
      Yes. For surface and minor pitting rust, we apply a rust neutralizer that
      converts active rust into a stable compound, then coat over it. More
      significant rust requires mechanical prep first. We inspect the vehicle
      and quote any additional prep work before starting — you'll never be
      surprised by charges.
  - question: Is dealer undercoating worth it?
    answer: >-
      Dealer undercoating is typically a thin spray applied in minutes — it's
      primarily a profit center for the dealer, not a serious rust protection
      solution. Professional undercoating from Capital Upfitters uses proper
      materials, applied with the right equipment, by specialists who do this
      every day. We see customers regularly who had dealer coating fail within
      two years. Don't waste the money — do it right.

## MARYLAND ROADS EAT METAL.

The DMV gets heavy salt treatment every winter. That salt works into every seam, every crevice, every surface your vehicle touches the road with. Undercoating is the only reliable defense.

Road salt accelerates corrosion dramatically. Our undercoating creates a sealed barrier between your vehicle's metal and the caustic salt and moisture that come with every winter.

Rubberized undercoating bonds to metal and cures into a thick, flexible shield that doesn't crack, peel, or wash away. Applied right, it lasts the life of the vehicle.

A welcome side effect: rubberized undercoating acts as a sound damper. Many customers notice a noticeably quieter ride after undercoating — especially on highway surfaces.

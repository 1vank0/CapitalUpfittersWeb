Professional detailing at your home, office, or fleet yard. Retail, fleet, and government accounts welcome. No need to drop off — we bring the equipment to you.

## CHOOSE YOUR PACKAGE.

Four packages built around what your vehicle actually needs. All mobile — our crew comes to you. Pricing varies by vehicle size (sedan, SUV, truck, van).

The essential interior clean — removes surface dirt, dust, and debris for a fresh cabin. Ideal for regular maintenance and fleet vehicle turnovers.

Deep interior restoration. Shampoo, steam, stain treatment, and leather care. Best for vehicles that need a real reset or pre-sale preparation.

A clean, protected exterior with attention to wheels, tires, and glass. Perfect for regular upkeep or fleet vehicle appearances.

## ENHANCE YOUR DETAIL.

Pair your detailing package with protection that lasts longer than any wax.

Pair your exterior detail with a professional ceramic coating application — applied by Capital Upfitters' certified installers (not the detailing crew). Nano-ceramic chemistry bonds to your paint for 2–5 year UV, chemical, and hydrophobic protection. The only add-on that outlasts every wax, sealant, or spray coating on the market.

Degreasing and detail clean of the engine bay. Removes oil residue, grime, and road buildup. Good for pre-sale prep or regular fleet maintenance documentation.

Eliminates stubborn interior odors from pets, smoke, food, and mildew at the molecular level. Effective where cleaning alone can't reach. 30-minute treatment per vehicle.
